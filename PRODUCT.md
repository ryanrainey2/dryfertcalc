# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Fertilizer retailers/dealers and independent agronomists. Retail staff use it to build and price dry and liquid fertilizer blends at the counter. Crop consultants use it to plan nutrient programs for grower accounts. Both need speed — decisions happen in conversation with a grower or on the go.

## Product Purpose

FertCalc Pro is a fertilizer blend calculator and agronomic planning tool. It lets users build custom dry or liquid blends, compute nutrient delivery per acre, optimize cost using nutrient-unit pricing, and generate print-ready reports. Supporting modules cover field management, soil tests, crop libraries, VRT (variable-rate technology) maps, 4R nutrient plans, a grower portal, inventory tracking, spreader calibration, and weather.

Success means a retailer can quote a blend in under a minute and an agronomist can hand a grower a complete nutrient plan without switching tools.

## Positioning

Speed and simplicity. FertCalc Pro is faster than spreadsheet-based alternatives or legacy dealer software. One screen, real-time math, no training required.

## Operating Context

Used on desktops at retail counters and on tablets/phones in the field. Often mid-conversation with a grower. Connectivity can be limited in rural areas. Users switch between dry and liquid modes, adjust prices to current market, save/load blends, and export PDF reports.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Dry and liquid product libraries with configurable pricing
- Real-time blend calculation with nutrient breakdown (N, P, K, S, B)
- Nutrient-unit cost optimization (residual/replacement method)
- Blend save/load via Supabase backend
- PDF export and print preview
- Multi-page app: Fields, Crops, Soil Tests, Inventory, Planner, Spreader, Weather, Nutrient Plan, VRT, Grower Portal, Feature Tracker, Admin
- Company-level settings with enabled product filtering
- Dark and light theme support
- No offline mode currently

## Product Principles

1. **Counter speed** — Every interaction must be completable in the time a grower is standing at the counter.
2. **Trust the math** — Nutrient calculations and cost optimization must be transparent and verifiable.
3. **Field-ready** — Works on mobile in poor connectivity; no friction between desktop and field use.
4. **One tool** — Blending, planning, and reporting in a single app, not scattered across spreadsheets.
5. **Retailer autonomy** — Not locked to any manufacturer's product line; works with whatever the dealer stocks.
